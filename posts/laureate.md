Nobody was handing out medals for "exemplary silence in a meeting that could have been an email", so I built a mint.

Laureate strikes a gold medal and diploma for extremely specific achievements, entirely in your browser. The press, the planchet, the relief, the ribbon, all drawn procedurally on canvas. You can drag the medal, flip it to read the engraving on the back, and share a permalink that re-mints the identical award.

No server, no database. The award lives in the URL.
